Meerut Weather: Sunny, 32°C Heat and Possible Thunderstorms

News · 15 Oct 2026

As the first half of October unfolds, residents of Meerut and nearby Muradabad are experiencing a brief spell of intense sunshine, with temperatures nudging the mid‑30s Celsius before a possible thunderstorm set to arrive tomorrow.

What happened

Local reports from Dainik Bhaskar indicate that today’s daytime heat in Meerut could climb to around 32 °C under clear skies. The same outlet notes that after two days of rain, the clouds have finally cleared, allowing the sun to dominate the day. In Muradabad, a similar pattern is observed: a cool breeze in the morning gives way to rising temperatures that could also peak near 32 °C, with a promise of cooler evenings.

Weather agencies are also warning of a shift in conditions tomorrow, when thunderstorms are expected to roll in across the region. The forecast mentions “गरज‑चमक के साथ बारिश” (rain with thunder and lightning) that could bring a brief respite from the heat but also raise concerns about sudden downpours.

Why it is trending

The combination of a sudden sunny spell after consecutive rainy days, a noticeable temperature rise, and the looming threat of thunderstorms has captured public attention on Google Trends. Searches for “weather Meerut” have surged, reflecting people’s need to plan daily activities, commute routes, and outdoor events. The trend also mirrors a broader seasonal shift: October marks the transition from the monsoon’s tail end to the cooler winter months in northern India, a period that often brings volatile weather patterns.

Key facts

  • Current temperature range: Up to 32 °C in Meerut and Muradabad during the day.
  • Morning conditions: Cooler breezes in Muradabad, while Meerut enjoys clear skies.
  • Rainfall forecast: Thunderstorms expected tomorrow, potentially bringing short, heavy showers.
  • Humidity: After two days of rain, humidity remains relatively high, making the heat feel more oppressive.
  • Seasonal context: October is a transitional month in Uttar Pradesh, often featuring a mix of sunny days and sudden rain showers as the monsoon withdraws.

What to watch next

Weather services will continue to update the outlook for the next 48 hours. Key indicators to monitor include:

  • Changes in temperature forecasts for the coming weekend.
  • Intensity and timing of the expected thunderstorms.
  • Potential impact on traffic and outdoor gatherings, especially in schools and markets.
  • Air quality levels, which can fluctuate with humidity and wind patterns.

Residents are advised to keep an eye on local bulletins, especially if they have plans that involve travel or outdoor work.

FAQ

Will the temperature drop after the thunderstorms?

Reports suggest that the rain and cloud cover associated with the thunderstorms could bring a temporary dip in temperature, offering brief relief from the 32 °C highs.

Is the humidity likely to stay high?

Given the recent rain and the current warm air, humidity is expected to remain elevated for the next couple of days, which may make the heat feel more uncomfortable.

Should I carry an umbrella tomorrow?

Yes, forecasts indicate a good chance of rain with thunder and lightning, so keeping an umbrella or raincoat handy is advisable.
