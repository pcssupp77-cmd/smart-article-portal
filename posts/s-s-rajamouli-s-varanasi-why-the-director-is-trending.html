S.S. Rajamouli’s Varanasi: Why the Director Is Trending

Entertainment · 12 Oct 2026

Veteran filmmaker S.S. Rajamouli has re‑entered the spotlight as several Indian news outlets report on his latest creative ventures. The buzz stems from his remarks about an upcoming project titled Varanasi and a candid comment on his long‑standing fascination with bringing the epic Ramayana to the silver screen. As a result, the director’s name is currently soaring on Google Trends India, prompting both fans and industry watchers to examine what lies ahead for the man behind Baahubali and RRR.

What happened

Three regional news portals highlighted different angles of Rajamouli’s recent activity. Eenadu quoted the director saying, “On the screen, the Ramayana… I have never been satisfied,” suggesting an ongoing ambition to adapt the ancient saga. Asianet News Telugu described Varanasi as “a brand‑new experiment in world cinema history,” emphasizing that the film’s core element—its setting—will be the city of Varanasi itself. Sakshi added a comparative note, asking whether audiences still remember the language of Baahubali, and hinted that Varanasi may surpass that benchmark.

Why it is trending

Google Trends data for India on 12 October 2026 shows the search term “ఎస్. ఎస్. రాజమౌళి” (S.S. Rajamouli) with over 200 searches per hour, a spike that aligns with the three headlines released within the same day. The convergence of a high‑profile director, a mysterious new film, and a bold statement about the Ramayana has ignited curiosity across social media platforms, fan forums, and industry newsletters. Moreover, Rajamouli’s reputation for pushing visual and narrative boundaries—evident in the record‑breaking Baahubali saga—means any hint of a new experiment automatically draws attention.

Key facts

  • Rajamouli reiterated his desire to portray the Ramayana on screen, saying he has never felt fully satisfied with past attempts.
  • The upcoming project is titled Varanasi, with reports suggesting the city itself will serve as a central narrative element.
  • Asianet News Telugu called the film “a brand‑new experiment in world cinema history.”
  • Sakshi compared the anticipated impact of Varanasi to the linguistic legacy of Baahubali.
  • Search interest in Rajamouli spiked to over 200 queries per hour on Google Trends India on 12 Oct 2026.

What to watch next

While official details remain limited, industry insiders expect the first look of Varanasi to emerge within the next few months, possibly accompanied by a teaser that showcases the city’s iconic ghats and cultural tapestry. Observers also anticipate that Rajamouli may collaborate with longtime composer M.M. Kreems or cinematographer K.K. Senthil Kumar, given their history on previous blockbusters. In parallel, the director’s earlier comment on the Ramayana could signal a future mythological venture, perhaps as a streaming series, a format that has gained traction among Indian audiences. Fans should keep an eye on official announcements from the director’s production house, as well as coverage from major Telugu and national media outlets.

FAQ

Is Varanasi Rajamouli’s first film set in a real Indian city?

Reports suggest that the film will be rooted in the actual city of Varanasi, using its geography and cultural landmarks as a narrative backdrop, marking a departure from the fictional kingdoms of his earlier epics.

Will the director finally make a full‑scale adaptation of the Ramayana?

Rajamouli has expressed ongoing interest in the epic, but no concrete production schedule has been announced. The recent comment indicates he remains unsatisfied with previous attempts and may pursue a new version in the future.

How does the buzz around Varanasi compare to the hype for his past projects?

While the anticipation for Baahubali and RRR was driven by massive marketing campaigns and confirmed release dates, the current excitement is more speculative, fueled by limited statements and the novelty of an experimental city‑centric story.
