Rain Today: Kerala Braces for Northeast Monsoon

News · 15 Oct 2026

Across the southern tip of India, the weather conversation has shifted from the lingering dryness of the southwest monsoon to an imminent spell of rain. The Hindu reports a 28% deficit in the southwest monsoon, prompting Kerala to brace for the northeast monsoon, while the India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued a four‑day heavy‑rain warning for Kerala, South Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Puducherry.

What happened

Recent observations show the southwest monsoon, which typically brings the bulk of the country’s monsoon rainfall, fell short of its usual volume by roughly a quarter. This shortfall has left large parts of Kerala with below‑average water tables and a heightened risk of drought. In response, the IMD has projected a trough moving eastward from the Bay of Bengal, expected to trigger widespread rainfall along the coastal belt of Tamil Nadu from the 14th onward, as noted by KWS Chennai. Simultaneously, BusinessLine relays that the IMD anticipates heavy rain over Kerala, South Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Puducherry for four consecutive days starting Thursday.

Why it is trending

The combination of a measurable monsoon deficit and an official heavy‑rain alert creates a perfect storm for public interest. Google Trends in India shows a spike in searches for “rain today,” reflecting immediate concerns about travel disruption, agricultural impact, and flood risk. Media outlets are amplifying the story because the expected rains could replenish reservoirs, but also pose challenges for a region still recovering from earlier dry spells.

Key facts

  • Southwest monsoon deficit: Approximately 28% below normal, according to The Hindu.
  • IMD forecast: Four days of heavy rain for Kerala, South Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Puducherry, starting Thursday.
  • Coastal Tamil Nadu: Widespread rainfall expected from the 14th, per KWS Chennai.
  • Potential impacts: Relief for water‑scarce areas, but heightened flood and landslide risk in low‑lying zones.
  • Public response: Surge in “rain today” searches, indicating widespread concern.

What to watch next

Observers will monitor the progression of the trough across the Bay of Bengal and its interaction with local topography. Key indicators include real‑time rainfall gauges from the IMD, updates from state disaster management agencies, and any advisories regarding road closures or school cancellations. In the longer term, the effectiveness of the northeast monsoon in offsetting the southwest deficit will shape water‑resource planning for the upcoming agricultural season.

FAQ

Will the rain cause flooding?

Reports suggest that heavy rain over four days could lead to localized flooding, especially in flood‑prone districts of Kerala and coastal Tamil Nadu. Residents are advised to stay updated with local alerts.

How will the rain affect agriculture?

The rainfall is expected to provide much‑needed moisture for paddy fields and cash crops that suffered during the dry southwest season, though timing and intensity will determine the overall benefit.

Should I change travel plans?

Given the forecast of widespread rain, it is prudent to check for road and rail service updates before traveling, particularly in coastal and hill‑area routes where landslides are possible.
