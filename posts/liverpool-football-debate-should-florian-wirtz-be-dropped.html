Liverpool football debate: Should Florian Wirtz be dropped?

Sports · 13 Oct 2026

Intro

Liverpool fans have found themselves at a crossroads as former England international Jamie Carragher and several pundits have publicly urged the club to bench German midfielder Florian Wirtz. The criticism has ignited a wave of discussion across social media, sports blogs, and mainstream outlets, turning the issue into one of the most searched terms on Google Trends India today.

What happened

In the past week three prominent headlines have captured the controversy. ESPN India reported that Carragher said Liverpool must drop Florian Wirtz, while The New York Times noted that the club “needed Florian Wirtz and Bradley Barcola to step up. They didn’t.” Goal.com added a more sensational angle, branding Wirtz as “the £116m signing on the chopping block” and quoting Carragher’s call for an immediate exclusion from the starting line‑up. No official statement from Liverpool has been released, but the repeated calls for action have put the player’s future under intense scrutiny.

Why it is trending

The surge in searches is driven by several factors. First, Wirtz arrived at Anfield with a hefty price tag that many fans still view as a benchmark for performance. Second, Liverpool’s recent form has been inconsistent, prompting supporters to look for scapegoats or catalysts for change. Third, Carragher’s reputation as a former Liverpool stalwart gives his opinion extra weight, especially when he appears on popular platforms like ESPN and Goal.com. Finally, the involvement of an international outlet such as The New York Times adds a global dimension, encouraging Indian readers to follow the story alongside the broader Premier League narrative.

Key facts

  • Florian Wirtz joined Liverpool for a reported £116 million fee.
  • Jamie Carragher has publicly called for Wirtz to be dropped from the squad.
  • The New York Times highlighted that both Wirtz and fellow signing Bradley Barcola have not met expectations.
  • Goal.com framed the situation as a potential “chopping block” scenario for the German midfielder.
  • Liverpool has not issued an official comment on the calls for Wirtz’s exclusion.

What to watch next

Fans should keep an eye on the next few fixtures, especially any matches where Liverpool’s midfield looks vulnerable. If the manager opts to rotate the squad, Wirtz’s playing time could be reduced, providing a clear signal about his standing. Transfer rumours may also resurface, with speculation that Liverpool could look to loan or sell the player if performances do not improve. Additionally, any follow‑up comments from Carragher or other pundits will likely influence public opinion and could affect the club’s internal decision‑making process.
