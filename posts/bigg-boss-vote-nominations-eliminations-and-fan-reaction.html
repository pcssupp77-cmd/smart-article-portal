FAQ

How does the voting process work?

Viewers can cast votes through the official Bigg Boss app, website, or designated SMS numbers. Each registered user is typically allowed one vote per day, though some platforms may permit multiple votes for a fee. The votes are tallied at the end of the voting window, and the contestant with the lowest count faces elimination.

Can fans influence the nominations?

Directly, no. Nominations are decided by the housemates based on internal dynamics and tasks. However, fan sentiment can indirectly affect future nominations, as contestants often adjust their behavior to appeal to the audience and avoid being put up for eviction.

When will the next elimination be announced?

Based on the show's usual schedule, the next elimination result is typically revealed within two to three days after the voting closes. Reports suggest that the producers will announce the outcome in the next episode, which is expected to air early next week.
