सलमान अली dazzles on Indian Idol, why he’s trending

Entertainment · 14 Oct 2026

Salman Ali, the 2023 Indian Idol winner, returned to the stage this week, and his appearance has quickly become a hot topic across India’s social media and news outlets.

What happened

During the latest episode of Indian Idol, the show featured a special segment that brought back several alumni from the series, including Abhijit Sawant, Amit Sana and the ever‑charismatic judge Himesh Reshammiya. In the midst of this nostalgic line‑up, Salman Ali performed a new rendition that reminded viewers of his signature “Goonj Dhurandhar” style. According to reports from AajTak, Himesh Reshammiya was visibly moved, even shedding tears, while fans took to Twitter and Instagram to share emotional reactions.

Why it is trending

The convergence of three factors has amplified the buzz:

  • Emotional resonance: Himesh’s reaction and the heartfelt response from long‑time viewers have created a viral moment.
  • Anniversary feel: The episode marked roughly twenty years since the original platform that launched many of these singers, a fact highlighted by Amar Ujala.
  • Social media amplification: Clips of Salman’s performance and Himesh’s reaction have been shared widely, leading to a spike in searches for “सलमान अली” on Google Trends.

Key facts

  • Salman Ali’s performance was part of a special alumni showcase on Indian Idol.
  • Himesh Reshammiya, a long‑time judge, was reported to have cried during the segment.
  • Abhijit Sawant and Amit Sana also returned to the stage, emphasizing the show’s 20‑year legacy.
  • Fans described the moment as “emotional” and “nostalgic” across multiple platforms.
  • Search interest for “सलमान अली” rose sharply, crossing the 200‑search threshold on Google Trends (IN).

What to watch next

Producers have hinted that the alumni segment is just the beginning of a larger “Indian Idol Legends” series. Viewers can expect:

  • More performances from past winners and runners‑up, potentially including new duets.
  • Behind‑the‑scenes interviews that explore each singer’s journey since their original appearance.
  • Special guest appearances by industry veterans who mentored the original contestants.

FAQ

Will Salman Ali release new music after his Indian Idol appearance?

Reports suggest that he is in talks with several music labels, but no official release date has been announced yet.

How many former contestants are featured in the upcoming “Legends” episodes?

According to the show’s producers, a total of ten alumni will be showcased over the next few weeks.

Is Himesh Reshammiya planning to return as a regular judge?

While his emotional response has reignited speculation, the network has not confirmed any changes to the judging panel.
