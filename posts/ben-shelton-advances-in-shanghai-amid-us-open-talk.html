Ben Shelton Advances in Shanghai Amid US Open Talk

Sports · 11 Oct 2026

American tennis star Ben Shelton is back in the spotlight after a convincing straight‑sets victory in Shanghai, reigniting discussions that began with his recent US Open final appearance.

What happened

According to reports from ESPN India, Shelton secured a straight‑sets win to advance in the Shanghai Masters, demonstrating the powerful serve and aggressive baseline play that have become his trademarks. The ATP Tour highlights confirm that he overcame Germany’s Dominik Altmaier in the second round, a match that showcased Shelton’s ability to dictate points early and maintain momentum throughout the contest. While exact scores were not disclosed, the consensus among observers is that Shelton’s performance was dominant, allowing him to move comfortably into the next round of the tournament.
