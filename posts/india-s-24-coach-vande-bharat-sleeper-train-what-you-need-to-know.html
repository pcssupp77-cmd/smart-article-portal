India’s 24‑Coach Vande Bharat Sleeper Train: What You Need to Know

News · 12 Oct 2026

Indian Railways is once again in the headlines as it moves from a 16‑coach Vande Bharat sleeper prototype to a longer 24‑coach version. The development has sparked a surge in online searches, making “24 coach Vande Bharat train” a trending term on Google Trends (IN). This article breaks down what the upgrade entails, why it matters, and what to expect in the coming months.

What happened

Following the launch of the first Vande Bharat sleeper train with 16 coaches, the rail ministry announced plans to introduce a 24‑coach variant. The Times of India reported that the new configuration is part of Indian Railways’ broader strategy to increase capacity on premium services while retaining the modern amenities that have defined the Vande Bharat brand.

NDTV highlighted the interior upgrades that will accompany the longer train, noting a “2AC Coach Tour” that showcases premium berths, panoramic windows and aircraft‑style toilets. These features aim to blend comfort with the high‑speed performance for which the Vande Bharat series is known.

Meanwhile, Deccan Herald raised concerns about the project’s timeline. The article points out that Kinetic (Kinet), the private partner responsible for the sleeper‑train conversion, could face a penalty if the rollout, which relies on Russian technology, slips further.

Why it is trending

Search interest spiked after the official announcement because the 24‑coach model promises a significant increase in passenger capacity—potentially accommodating more travelers on high‑demand routes. Social media discussions have also focused on the train’s upgraded amenities and the implications for ticket pricing.

Industry observers are watching how the longer train will affect platform lengths, maintenance schedules, and the overall rollout of Vande Bharat services across the network. The combination of capacity, comfort and the involvement of a private‑public partnership adds layers of interest for both rail enthusiasts and regular commuters.

Key facts

  • Current configuration: 16‑coach sleeper Vande Bharat train already in service.
  • Planned upgrade: 24‑coach version announced by Indian Railways.
  • Premium features: 2AC berths, panoramic windows, aircraft‑style toilets as shown in NDTV’s coach tour.
  • Partnership: Kinetic (Kinet) handling conversion, using Russian technology.
  • Potential risk: Penalty risk for Kinetic if project delays continue, per Deccan Herald.

What to watch next

Analysts suggest three key developments will shape the story in the coming weeks:

  • Testing and certification: The 24‑coach train will need to undergo safety and performance trials before it can enter commercial service.
  • Infrastructure readiness: Platforms and maintenance depots may require upgrades to handle the longer trainsets.
  • Pricing and ticketing: Indian Railways will likely announce fare structures that reflect the added capacity and premium amenities.

Stakeholders will also be monitoring any regulatory actions related to Kinetic’s timeline, as penalties could impact the overall rollout schedule.

FAQ

Will the 24‑coach Vande Bharat sleeper be more expensive?

While exact fare details have not been released, reports suggest that the premium amenities and increased capacity could lead to a modest price adjustment compared with the 16‑coach version.

How will the longer train affect travel times?

The Vande Bharat platform is designed for high‑speed operation, and adding coaches does not inherently change the train’s top speed. However, longer dwell times at stations may be observed as more passengers board and alight.

What routes are likely to see the 24‑coach version first?

Indian Railways has not confirmed specific routes, but high‑traffic corridors such as Delhi‑Mumbai and Chennai‑Kolkata are often early candidates for capacity‑boosting upgrades.
